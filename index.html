PensionsKalkITP1

Calculations of different interest with regards to Swedish pension.

Available Tools

  • Financial Projection Visualize your financial timeline leading up to your chosen retirement age. Projects accumulated income, net living costs, severance coverage, and the growth of your pensions across key life milestones.
  • Löneväxling Calculator Calculate the guaranteed tax arbitrage profit when exchanging salary (löneväxling). Compares taking the salary today vs. saving it for retirement with employer bonuses.
  • Pension Allocation Breakdown See exactly where your pension money goes. Breaks down your monthly and yearly funding into Allmän Pension, Premiepension (PPM), Tjänstepension (ITP1), and Löneväxling.
