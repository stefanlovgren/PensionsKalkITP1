How Calculations Work

Overall Purpose

  • Financial Projection: This tool helps you visualize your financial timeline leading up to your chosen Projection End Age. It projects accumulated income, net living costs, and the growth of your pensions across key life milestones.
  • Runway Analysis: By comparing your accumulated costs against a potential net severance payout, you can see exactly when your severance money might run out.

Budget Calculator

  • Daily Cost Estimation: Click the calculator icon (🖩) next to "Daily Cost" to break down your monthly expenses. The tool will convert this into a daily burn rate.
  • Presets & Customization: You can load standard Swedish budget profiles, tweak the numbers, and save your own custom presets directly to your browser for future use.

Timeline & Milestones

  • Cost Target Age: The age you want to project your remaining living expenses towards. If the timeline extends past this age, the remaining cost will count upwards with a +.
  • Cost Interval (SEK): Sets a recurring financial milestone. A highlighted row is added to the timeline exactly when your projected remaining costs hit a multiple of this amount.
  • Toggle Milestones: Use the checkboxes under "Timeline Options" to optionally hide or show specific chronological events (Birthdays, New Year, 1 Year, 1 Month) and recurring financial milestones (Cost, Severance).
  • Custom Events: Click the "✎ Edit Events" button to open the event manager and add specific dates/events to your timeline.

Export & Import

  • Backup Settings: Use the green Export button to download all your inputs, budget presets, and custom events as a JSON file. Use the purple Import button to restore them later.

Tax Rules (Gothenburg 2026 rules)

  • Municipal Tax: 33.20% (Göteborg).
  • State Tax: 20% on taxable income above 625,600 SEK.
  • Deductions: Automatically calculates Basic Deduction (Grundavdrag) and Earned Income Tax Credit (Jobbskatteavdrag) using 2026 baselines (Prisbasbelopp = 58,800 SEK).

Pension Rules (Standard 2024)

  • Public Pension (Allmän pension): 18.5% of pensionable income, capped at an income ceiling of 614,934 SEK/year.
  • Occupational Pension (Tjänstepension - ITP1): 4.5% on salary up to 571,500 SEK/year, 30% on salary above that ceiling, plus 1.7% flexpension on the entire gross amount.
  • Löneväxling (Salary Exchange): Extra pension contribution deducted from your gross salary before income tax calculation, effectively lowering your accumulated net income and taxes while boosting your pension.

Severance

  • Severance: Calculated as a net lump sum payout based on your base monthly gross salary (ignoring löneväxling). It is subtracted from your accumulated costs to show when the money runs out.
